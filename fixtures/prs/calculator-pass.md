## Summary
Adds integer division to the calculator.

## Requirements
1. `Calculator.add` returns the sum of two integers: adding 40 and 2 returns 42.
2. `Calculator.divide` returns the truncated quotient: dividing 7 by 2 returns 3.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC: Given 40 and 2, add returns 42.
- AC: Given 7 and 2, divide returns 3.
